## Memorizing Rules

### Cut & Don't Cut

It's pretty much just straight memorization, but the two wires that can be memorized together
are

> Red Star = Cut<br />
> Blue Star = Don't Cut

Other than that make sure you memorize that White and White Star are cut. It's not as important
to memorize the other Don't Cuts since you shouldn't cut a wire if none of the other rules
apply, but another easy one to memorize is don't cut when everything is true (LED Red Blue
Star).

### Serial Even

This rule applies for wires 1-3. Cut when the color is not white (red, blue, or both) and there
is no LED and no star. The Red Blue rule covers the 4th wire.

### Red Blue

From the Serial Even rule we know to cut the Red Blue wire if the serial is even. If the LED is
on (with no Star), still cut if the serial is even. Otherwise if there is a Star (with no LED),
cut if there's a parallel port.

### Parallel Port & Batteries

I found it easiest to memorize these together. For this rule, the LED must be on and the Star
is optional. Just remember:

> Blue = Parallel Port<br />
> Red = Batteries

This covers wires 1-2 for Parallel Port and Batteries. Wire 3 of Parallel Port is covered by
the Red Blue rule, and wire 3 of Batteries is an exception that must be memorized.
