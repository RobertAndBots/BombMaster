Six columns, seven symbols each — but only **27 distinct symbols** across those 42 slots. So
some symbols appear twice and some appear exactly once, and that asymmetry is the module.

## Twelve symbols identify the column on sight

These appear in exactly one column. See any of them and you are done searching:

<KeypadSets show="unique" />

The other fifteen appear in two columns, so they narrow it to a pair and you need a second
symbol to decide:

<KeypadSets show="shared" />

In practice I scan the four symbols for one from the first list. Most modules have one, and
when they do the lookup is instant.

## Mnemonics for each column

The symbols have no names the game agrees with, so you have to invent them. What worked for
me was a sentence per column where each word stands for one symbol **in order** — reciting
the sentence gives you the press order for free, which is the actual thing being asked.

<KeypadSets show="sets" />

Some of these are a stretch ("Sax-E, not a try, no-mega") and that is fine — a bad pun you
can't forget beats an elegant one you can. Write your own if mine don't land; the useful part
is the structure, not my words.
