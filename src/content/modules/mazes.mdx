Mazes is the odd one out: there is no table and no mnemonic. There are nine layouts, the
module shows you none of the walls, and you have to know which of the nine you're in from two
circle markers and then navigate it blind.

So this page is mostly a tool rather than an explanation.

## How to actually learn them

Reading the nine mazes off the manual does very little. What worked was **walking them with
the walls hidden** and only revealing when I got stuck — the same way you learn a route by
driving it rather than by looking at a map.

The trainer below does that. Pick a maze, navigate to the target, and hit **Show walls** when
you're lost. As you improve you'll find you're peeking at one or two corners of each maze
rather than the whole thing, and those corners are the bit worth drilling.

A few things that helped:

- **Learn the circles first.** Identifying the maze is a separate skill from navigating it,
  and it's much easier — nine pairs of dots. Get that instant, then worry about walls.
- **Learn the edges and the middle separately.** Most of these mazes have an obvious
  perimeter route and a messy centre. The perimeter is cheap to learn and often enough.
- **Walking into a wall is free here.** The bump flashes red and costs you nothing but a tick
  on the counter, which is exactly the feedback you want while learning. It is not free in
  game.

## Trainer

Click the board first, then use the arrow keys or WASD. **Space** toggles the walls. On a
phone, use the arrows below the grid.
