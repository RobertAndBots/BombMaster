For Memory, the rules can refer to buttons by:

1. (P#) Position
2. (L4) Label = "4"
3. (S#P) Stage 1 & 2 position
4. (S#L) Stage 1-4 label

D# = "display is #"

## Memorizing

Stage 1 is pretty straightforward to memorize since display 2-4 → position 2-4. You just need
to remember display 1 → position 2.

For stage 2, I remember displays 2 and 4 as the "easy" displays since it makes the in-game
memorizing easier as there is now only one position to memorize (since stages 1 and 2 will
have the same position). For displays 3 and 1, I remember 314: display 3 → position 1,
display 1 → label 4. You can remember this by thinking of pi: 3.14.

For stage 3, I remember 3-3-3: stage 3, display 3, position 3. You can then remember display 4,
label 4. Then displays 1 and 2 are the labels from 1 and 2, just switched.

I remember stage 4 as mostly positions from other stages. Everything before display 2 is
stage 1 position, everything after is stage 2 position, and display 2 → position 1.

Stage 5 is only labels from other stages. Each display corresponds to which stage the label is
from, except 3 and 4 are switched

## In-game Memory

When you're defusing this module in-game, you need to remember the labels for stages 1-4 and
the positions for stages 1-2. I remember this as three 2-digit numbers:

1. Stage 1 position, stage 2 position
2. Stage 1 label, stage 2 label
3. Stage 4 label, stage 3 label

Notice that for the third number I put stage 4 before stage 3. This makes stage 5 easier for
me so I don't have to flip the labels after the display shows. For you it might be easier to
remember all the labels individually or as a 4-digit number. Experiment and see what works
best for you.

In stage 4, once you decide the position of the button to click, you can immediately forget
about stage 1 and 2 positions and just focus on the labels of the previous stages, so you can
complete stage 5 as fast as possible.

In stage 2, if you get display 2 or 4, you only have to remember one position since stage 1
and 2 will have the same position.
