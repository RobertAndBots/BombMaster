Step one is learning Morse code itself, which this site doesn't teach — there are phone apps
built for exactly that and they work better than anything I could put here. You only need the
letters, and only the ones these sixteen words use.

## Don't memorize the frequencies

This is the shortcut that makes the module easy. The sixteen words are in a **fixed order**
and the frequencies ascend with them. So you don't need the numbers — you need the word list.
Once you've decoded the word, say the list in your head and click the dial up that many
times.

I did eventually pick up **flick = 3.555**, because it's the ninth word. If the word is in the
back half I jump straight there instead of stepping through the first eight.

## The words

Four groups of four. For the first group I use **"she has slick trick"** — shell, halls,
slick, trick. For the rest I just learned the words themselves; they're short and they don't
collide much.

The decoding trap worth knowing: several words differ by one letter near the end
(**break** / **brick**, **steak** / **sting**). Wait for the full word rather than committing
on the first three letters — there's a long gap before it repeats, and guessing costs you the
whole loop.
