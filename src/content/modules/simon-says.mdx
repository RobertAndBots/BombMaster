For simon says, the color mapping depends on how many strikes you have and whether or not
there's a vowel in the serial number. Starting out you really only have to memorize the two
mappings for when there are no strikes.

## Vowel, No Strikes

Red and Blue are flipped<br />
Green and Yellow are flipped

## No Vowel, No Strikes

Red, Blue, and Yellow rotate<br />
Green is Green

## No Vowel, 1 Strike

Green and Yellow are flipped<br />
Red is Red, Blue is Blue

## Vowel, 1 Strike & No Vowel, 2 Strikes

Red and Yellow are flipped<br />
Blue and Green are flipped

## Vowel, 2 Strikes

Rotate counter-clockwise
