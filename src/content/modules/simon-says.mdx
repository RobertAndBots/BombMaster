The colour you press depends on two things: whether the serial number contains a **vowel**,
and how many **strikes** you already have. Six tables in all.

Starting out you really only need the two no-strike mappings. If you have a strike you
already have bigger problems, and you can look those two up.

<SimonMappings />

## How I hold each one

- **Vowel, no strikes** — red and blue swap; green and yellow swap.
- **No vowel, no strikes** — red, blue and yellow rotate (R→B→Y→R); green stays green.
- **No vowel, 1 strike** — red stays red and blue stays blue; green and yellow swap.
- **Vowel, 1 strike** *and* **no vowel, 2 strikes** — the same table: red and yellow swap,
  blue and green swap.
- **Vowel, 2 strikes** — rotate counter-clockwise around the module's own layout.

The one genuinely useful structural fact is that **vowel + 1 strike and no-vowel + 2 strikes
are identical**, so six tables is really five.

> **Correction, September 2026.** An earlier version of this page said the no-vowel,

## In game

Say the mapping out loud once before you start pressing. The sequence lengthens by one each
round and replays from the beginning, so a wrong mapping does not cost you one press — it
costs you the whole module, repeatedly, until someone notices.
