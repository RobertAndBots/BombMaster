## Step 1

Here are the lists of words sorted by position. Note that {empty} signifies when the display
does not show a word.

<WhosOnFirstStep1 />

To memorize them, I grouped similar words together and created some rules:

1. <WhosOnFirstGrid cells={['UR', 'CFO', 'Yes, Led, Nothing', 'red, you', 'eed', '']} />

   **Bottom Left:** eed → Leed/Reed

   **Middle Right:** red → Red/Read, and you (no spaces) → You/Your/You're (**not** You are)

2. They are/Their/They're form a triangle:

   <WhosOnFirstGrid highlight cells={['', '', 'They are', 'Their', "They're", '']} />

   You can optionally add **There** and make it a square since it's in Bottom Right.

3. The "blanks" (Blank/{empty}):

   <WhosOnFirstGrid highlight cells={['', '', '', 'Blank', empty, '']} />

4. Everything else is Bottom Right

## Step 2

There are 28 words that can be split in half into two groups. The word lists for each group of
14 only pull from those 14 words.

Also remember that you only have to memorize [at most the first 9
words](https://www.reddit.com/r/ktane/comments/3uirhw/update_on_whos_on_first_memorization_rediscovery/cxgaatu/)
in a word list, and you can also stop when you see the step 1 word itself (For example, for
Left only memorize Right, Left).
